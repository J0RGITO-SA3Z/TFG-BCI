\chapter*{Abstract}

\section*{\tituloPortadaEngVal}

Video games are a very common pastime today. Unfortunately, there are people
unable to play them due to their mobility limitations. There are solutions such
as the \textit{``quadstick''}\footnote{\url{https://www.quadstick.com}}, which
allow these users to play using their mouth. However, this control is not very
intuitive or practical. An alternative would be making it possible to play a
game by thinking about what one wants to do.

The reading of brain waves through electroencephalography (EEG) has existed for
years; recently, its integration with artificial intelligence (AI) models has
opened new ways to interpret them. To date, brain-computer interfaces (BCI)
have proven to be impractical, whether due to their dependence on external
stimuli, their high cost, or their low portability.

This work aims to design and implement a modular BCI system capable of
processing EEG signals in real time and translating them into commands to
control video games. The system uses a lightweight and affordable
electroencephalograph alongside the MiRepNet deep learning model, adapted
through \textit{fine-tuning} techniques to capture the particularities of each
user.

Experimental results show an average accuracy of 80\% in the binary
classification of MI (right hand and left hand) on recorded data
(\textit{offline}), reaching values above 90\% in subjects with high signal
quality.

To validate the system in a dynamic context, two video games (\textit{Endless
    Wave Runner} and \textit{Project Arrows}) have been developed, integrating
decision filters and assistance mechanisms. These allow for compensating the
variability of brain signals, achieving a smooth and pleasant user experience.

Experiments testing the system demonstrated that controlling video games
through thought is possible with accessible hardware. For the most part, users
who tested the system were happy with it, reporting that they were satisfied
with the experience and that playing with their minds had been both fun and
entertaining. \section*{Keywords}
